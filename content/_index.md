**Broken Gadgets R&D** is an independent hardware, firmware, and infrastructure engineering lab focused on custom electronics, embedded control systems, self-hosted security tooling, and reverse engineering.

### Focus Areas & Open-Source Work

- **Infrastructure & Access Control** — Lightweight edge authentication and network plumbing, including [`haproxy-github-oauth`](https://github.com/brokengadgets/haproxy-github-oauth) for gating VPN-backed internal services behind HAProxy Lua JWT verification and GitHub Team ACLs.
- **Embedded Hardware & Instrumentation** — Custom KiCad PCBs, microcontroller firmware, and lab control interfaces such as [`OpenMS-controller`](https://github.com/brokengadgets/OpenMS-controller) *(WIP)*.
- **Build Logs & Technical Notes** — Practical writeups covering hardware bring-up, latency-sensitive controller design, and low-maintenance self-hosted infrastructure.

[Reach out via our contact page](/contact/) if you think Broken Gadgets R&D can help with your project, or explore our open-source repositories on [GitHub](https://github.com/brokengadgets).
